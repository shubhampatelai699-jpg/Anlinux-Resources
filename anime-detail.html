Tanjiro Kamado is a kind-hearted boy who sells charcoal for a living. After returning home one day, he discovers his family has been slaughtered by demons — and his younger sister Nezuko has been turned into one. Driven by the desire to avenge his family and restore Nezuko's humanity, Tanjiro joins the Demon Slayer Corps. Season 4 — Hashira Training Arc — follows Tanjiro as he trains under each of the nine Hashira to prepare for the inevitable final battle against Muzan Kibutsuji and his army inside the Infinity Castle.
