Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you reach out.

Is Indian One Anime free?

Yes! Everything on Indian One Anime is completely free — no sign-up, no subscription required.

How do I download the Dubkami app?

Visit the Dubkami page and download the APK directly. Enable "Install from unknown sources" in your Android settings to install it.

A video player isn't working — what do I do?

Try switching to another player using the Player 1 / Player 2 / Player 3 tabs on the watch page. If all players fail, use the contact form above to report it.

Can I request an anime to be added?

Absolutely! Use the form above and select "Anime Request" as the subject. We try to fulfil popular requests as quickly as possible.

How fast are new episodes added?

We aim to add new episodes as soon as they air. Check the New Releases page for the latest drops.
